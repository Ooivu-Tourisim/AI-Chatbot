1. Brand concept and website promise
Position the business as an experience-based Northern Sri Lanka tourism service: not only sightseeing, but also village life, traditional food, farming, fishing, crafts, island trips, photography, religious heritage and personalised celebrations.
Suggested tagline: “Discover the North. Live the Stories. Take Home the Memories.”
Coverage: Jaffna and nearby islands, Kilinochchi, Mullaitivu, Mannar and Vavuniya. Build district-specific routes and avoid promising that all districts can be comfortably covered in a three-day trip.

9. Suggested pricing and profit method
All prices in this catalogue are indicative planning ranges, not verified current supplier quotations. Before publishing, request written rates from operators and calculate the complete cost per booking.
A simple pricing method: Selling price = total direct cost ÷ (1 − target gross margin). For example, if direct cost is LKR 10,000 and the target gross margin is 25%, the selling price is LKR 13,333 before any additional taxes or costs not included in the direct-cost figure.
Use a per-person rate for shared group tours and a per-couple/per-group price for private experiences. State minimum group size, child rates, single-room supplements, taxes, exclusions and cancellation terms.

11. Safety, respect and responsible tourism
•	Boat and lagoon activities must use suitable vessels, qualified operators, life jackets and weather checks.
•	Do not promise open-sea proposal setups or landings without permission and a safe operating plan.
•	Get consent before photographing residents, workers, children or religious ceremonies.
•	Respect temple, church, mosque and archaeological-site rules, dress codes and prayer/service times.
•	For farming and harvesting, work around actual planting and harvest seasons and obtain landowner consent.
•	For crab and prawn activities, follow local fishing rules, farm hygiene and safe food handling.
•	For bullock-cart rides, use humane handling, safe loads, shade, rest and suitable routes.
•	Offer drinking water, first-aid arrangements, emergency contacts and clear accessibility information.
•	Pay community hosts fairly and agree in advance on responsibilities, rates and customer limits.
12. Supplier checklist before publishing
•	Boat operators: licence/authorisation, passenger capacity, life jackets, weather policy and insurance.
•	Farmers and village hosts: season, group capacity, activity duration, footwear and hygiene arrangements.
•	Fishers / seafood hosts: legal location, catch availability, cooking facilities, allergy and food safety arrangements.
•	Accommodation: verified photos, room types, location, included meals, cancellation terms and taxes.
•	Guides and transport: language, vehicle capacity, rates, waiting time and emergency contacts.
•	Photographers and event decorators: portfolio, deliverables, privacy, setup permissions and cancellation terms.
•	Religious and heritage sites: access, hours, entry fees, dress rules and photography restrictions.
•	Written supplier agreement: rates, payment timing, customer responsibilities, refunds and service standards.
